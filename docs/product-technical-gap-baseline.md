## DDD, context map and persistence contract

Core subdomain: **Policy Fact Authoring**. Supporting: **Review & Publication** and **Legal Source Registry**. Generic identity, workflow, provider and observability services do not own product facts. `Policy Fact Authoring -> Review & Publication` supplies a versioned fact contract. `Legal Source Registry -> Review & Publication` supplies source/rule revisions through an ACL. External customer/source-system adapters do not mutate foreign systems through hidden coupling.

Ubiquitous language: `policy_revision`, `service_profile`, explicit no-collection attestation, `retention_status`, `collection_item`, `processing_purpose`, `retention_rule`, `third_party_transfer`, `international_transfer`, `privacy_contact`, `review_finding`, `legal_source_revision`, `publication_revision`. The Proposed minimal aggregate is revision-owned facts under `policy_revision`. Categorical predicates are domain admission rules, not a generic external schema authority or a new aggregate.

Collection and retention remain independent revision-owned facts. `retention_rule` exists only for `retention_status = applies`. When transitioning away from `applies`, delete the current `retention_rule` in the same transaction. A retained `retention_rule` causes the deferred constraint to reject the commit. Immutable audit history remains separate. Preserve revision ownership, tenant-scoped version uniqueness, 3NF, parent-row lock coordination, complete item-key UPSERT/idempotency and NULL-safe restored-value assertions. Rendered prose is a read projection, not an authority store.

Identity, ontology, translation and other ecosystem consumers use released versioned contracts with ACLs only. An immature owner remains an owner-side development target; until release, keep integration behind ports/feature flags/test doubles. No copied supplier source, cross-service SQL, temporary-branch dependency or unapproved publication is admitted.
